Chapter 3 · The Set-Aside Moat

Small-business set-asides narrow the field - if you qualify

What set-asides are

Some federal contracts are reserved for small businesses or specific programs (VOSB/SDVOSB, WOSB, 8(a), HUBZone). Larger firms that don't meet the size or program rules generally can't compete for those specific awards, so the field is often smaller. Bidder counts vary and aren't guaranteed.

Eligibility requires certification

Veteran-owned programs require VA/SBA certification; WOSB and 8(a) require SBA certification. Check your eligibility before targeting them.

Proof over fluff

Chapter 4 · Deconstructing the Scope of Work

The four gates that break when you leave the van

Gate 1: Blind phone quotes on bad paint

Quoting sight-unseen destroys margin when the crew finds oxidation, sap or heavy pet hair.

Gate 2: Pre-existing damage disputes

Without arrival photos, one false curb-rash claim wipes out a week's profit.

Gate 3: Quality drift

If you personally inspect every car, you're an unpaid quality supervisor.

Gate 4: Missed calls with wet hands

Waiting hours to text a lead back loses the best ceramic and fleet clients.
